How we research, score, and update our guides · Updated November 11, 2026

This page explains how BioSavvy researches, scores, and updates the product guides published on this site.

1. Research first

Before writing a guide, we gather:

  • Manufacturer specifications, manuals, and feature documentation
  • Materials and published safety certifications where available
  • Warranty and return terms
  • Published research related to the product category and its claims
  • Verified customer feedback from major marketplaces

2. Head-to-head comparison

We compare products within the same category on criteria that matter to buyers: performance, build quality, safety, ease of use, and value for money. Comparisons are relative to the products included in each guide.

3. Transparent scoring

Each product is scored across four dimensions:

  • Performance — how well it does its core job, based on specifications and reported user experience
  • Value — price relative to features and build quality
  • Safety — certifications, materials, and built-in safeguards
  • Durability — materials, construction, and warranty support

Scores reflect the products compared in that guide. They are not absolute laboratory ratings.

4. What “reviewed” means

Unless a guide explicitly states otherwise, our recommendations are based on research and specification comparison, not laboratory testing or long-term hands-on use. We avoid implying first-hand testing where none took place.

5. Sources and verification

We prioritize primary sources: manufacturer documentation, regulatory databases, and peer-reviewed research. Marketplace reviews are used as supporting signals, with attention to verified purchases and consistent patterns rather than individual anecdotes.

6. Updates and corrections

Guides are dated and updated when prices, specifications, or product availability change. Factual errors are corrected promptly. To report an error, contact us with the article URL and the specific claim in question.

7. Independence

We do not accept payment for reviews or rankings. Affiliate commissions do not influence our recommendations or scores. See our affiliate disclosure.

8. Medical disclaimer

Our content is for general information only and is not medical advice. Health devices and supplements are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting any new health product or routine.
