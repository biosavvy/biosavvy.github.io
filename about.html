About BioSavvy

Independent product research · Updated November 11, 2026

BioSavvy is an independent review publication covering health, beauty, fitness, and home-wellness products. Our goal is simple: help readers cut through marketing hype and choose products that are genuinely worth their money.

What we cover

We publish buying guides and product comparisons in four main areas:

  • Smart health gadgets and monitoring devices
  • Beauty tech and at-home skincare devices
  • Home fitness equipment and recovery tools
  • Ergonomic and everyday wellness products

Our guides focus on products that are widely available online to shoppers in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia.

How we research products

Every guide follows the same repeatable process:

  1. Research. We collect manufacturer specifications, materials, warranty terms, and the published evidence behind each product’s claims.
  2. Compare. Products are benchmarked head-to-head on measurable criteria, including performance, build quality, safety features, and value for money.
  3. Score. Each product receives a transparent score across performance, value, safety, and durability.
  4. Recommend. We publish honest picks with clear pros, cons, and a note on who each product is best for.

An important note on testing. We do not claim to have laboratory-tested or long-term tested every product we write about. Unless a guide explicitly says otherwise, our recommendations are based on research and specification comparison, not hands-on laboratory testing. Read our full editorial policy.

Editorial independence

BioSavvy does not accept payment for reviews, rankings, or placements. Some links on this site are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission if you buy through them — at no extra cost to you. Affiliate relationships never determine which products we recommend or how we rank them. See our affiliate disclosure.

Corrections and updates

Product specifications, prices, and availability change frequently. We review and update our guides regularly, and we correct factual errors when they are brought to our attention. If you spot a mistake, please contact us.

Who we are

BioSavvy is run by a small independent editorial team. We are not a manufacturer, retailer, or medical provider, and we do not sell any of the products featured on this site.
